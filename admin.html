Your letters

Each registration goes to three of you, picked at random for it, a team’s members to the same three. One letter at a time: grade it from 1 to 10 (the keys 1 to 9, and 0 for 10), S to skip it for now. You see the others’ grades once you have given yours. Your grades wait on this page and go together when you press Send, at each pause (every 20 letters) and when you close the letters.

Click any bar below to keep only those people, and more bars to narrow it down. The other bars, the chart, the table, Copy emails and the CSV follow.

Registrations per day

Decision emails

You grade from 1 to 10 in Your letters above (or in the table): once 3 of you have, the average makes the decision (7 or more: accepted, 5 or more: the waiting list, below: denied), and that only notes it. Sort the table by Grade for the highest averages first. Nothing goes out until you press Send, which first shows who gets what. Google allows about 100 emails a day: the accepted go first, then the waiting list, then the denied, and the next day’s Send carries on. Registration closes on 18 October at 23:59.

Teams

Each participant sees their team on the dashboard: its name, challenge, teammates and pitch time. Make teams from the registrations puts the accepted people who registered together into a team each. The place (1st, 2nd, 3rd in its challenge) goes on the certificates, and the team sees it after the awards.

    Who can open it

    The accepted participants only, each with the personal code from their acceptance email, at roqteam.ro/teams. Changing someone’s decision shuts their code out at once. Someone lost the email? Their code is in their full view: Registrations, then their name. Organisers open it with the admin key.

    Mentors

    The teams press “Ask a mentor” on the dashboard; the mentors see the requests, oldest first, at roqteam.ro/mentors, take one and mark it done. Send the mentors their link: it carries the mentor key, which opens the queue and nothing else. A new key shuts the old link out at once.

    Big screen

    For the smart board or the laptop on the projector, at roqteam.ro/screen: what is on now and next, the countdown to the end of coding, the newest announcements, how many teams wait for a mentor and, at the awards, the audience vote. It opens with its own password, so the admin key need not go on a shared screen; the password shows the screen and nothing else. The button on it, or F, makes it full screen.
