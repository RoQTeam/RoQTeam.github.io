Decision emails

Accept, Waitlist and Deny only note the decision. Nothing goes out until you press Send, which first shows who gets what. Google allows about 100 emails a day: the accepted go first, then the waiting list, then the denied, and the next day’s Send carries on. Registration closes on 18 October at 23:59.

Teams

Each participant sees their team on the dashboard: its name, challenge, table, teammates and pitch time. Make teams from the registrations puts the accepted people who registered together into a team each. The place (1st, 2nd, 3rd in its challenge) goes on the certificates, and the team sees it after the awards.

    Who can open it

    The accepted participants only, each with the personal code from their acceptance email, at roqteam.ro/teams. Changing someone’s decision shuts their code out at once. Someone lost the email? Their code is in their full view: Registrations, then their name. Organisers open it with the admin key.

    Mentors

    The teams press “Ask a mentor” on the dashboard; the mentors see the requests, oldest first, at roqteam.ro/mentors, take one and mark it done. Send the mentors their link: it carries the mentor key, which opens the queue and nothing else. A new key shuts the old link out at once.
