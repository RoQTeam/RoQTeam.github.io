BreaQ hackathon · Challenge 02

Entangled Learning

Quantum machine learning with PennyLane

BreaQ 202624‑hour hackathon, Bucharest PennyLane · PythonQuantum and hybrid models Register

The challenge

Quantum machine learning encodes data into the state of a quantum system, where a few qubits span a feature space that grows exponentially with their number. The promise: correlations a classical model cannot reach cheaply become visible there.

Does that promise survive contact with a real dataset? That is the question this challenge asks.

You will build, train and evaluate quantum and hybrid quantum‑classical models in PennyLane on a dataset chosen to be hard for classical learners, and make a clear, evidence‑backed case for where the quantum model helps, where it does not, and why.

What a strong entry looks like

  • A solid classical baseline, so that the comparison means something.
  • At least one purely quantum model and one hybrid model, with the data encoding explained and justified.
  • An honest analysis: accuracy, training cost, sensitivity to noise, and the role entanglement actually played.
  • Code somebody else can run, and a short write‑up of what you found.
When
During BreaQ 2026, dates to be announced. Twenty‑four hours of coding, then presentations to the jury.
Where
Bucharest. The venue is confirmed together with the dates.
Tools
PennyLane and Python. Any classical library for baselines and for the classical half of hybrid models.
Format
24 hours in teams, then a public repository and a short presentation to the jury.

What you get

  • Mentors in the room

  • Your laptop is enough
