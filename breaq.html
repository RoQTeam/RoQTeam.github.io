About BreaQ

BreaQ is RoQTeam’s annual gathering for Romania’s quantum community: a 24‑hour hackathon and a day of talks by leading researchers. Students, researchers and the simply curious are all welcome.

What to Expect

  • Presentations & workshops

    Talks by researchers and industry experts on quantum computing, communication and sensing, with workshops that put the ideas to work.

  • Quantum hackathon

    Twenty‑four hours in teams on real challenges, with mentors on the floor and prizes at the end.

  • Networking

    Meet fellow enthusiasts, collaborators and future employers. Connections that last beyond the event horizon.
