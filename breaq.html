About BreaQ

BreaQ is RoQTeam’s annual gathering for Romania’s quantum community, in two parts: the BreaQ Hackathon, where teams take on real quantum challenges, and the BreaQ Conference, a day of talks by leading researchers. Students, researchers and the simply curious are all welcome.

BreaQ Conference

Military Technical Academy “Ferdinand I”, Bucharest

Three weeks after the hackathon, BreaQ moves on stage: a day of talks by researchers and industry experts. Open to everyone, whether or not you hacked.
