Last year’s challenges

At the first BreaQ, in November 2025, every team picked one of three challenges:

Quantum machine learning

Entangled Learning

Participants were challenged to develop hybrid quantum–classical neural networks for grading diabetic retinopathy from RetinaMNIST medical images. The task involved designing new architectures and experiments to assess whether quantum components improve predictive performance, reduce the number of trainable parameters, or enable better generalization from limited training data compared with classical models.

Built with Classiq

Quantum Codebreaking

Participants were challenged to implement the Kuperberg sieve algorithm using Classiq, combining quantum operations with classical post‑processing to recover a hidden parameter in the Dihedral Hidden Subgroup Problem. The task explored how quantum algorithms can tackle hard problems relevant to cryptanalysis, with an additional challenge to adapt the implementation for execution on quantum hardware.

Quantum key distribution

Quantum Hacking

Participants were challenged to simulate attacks on quantum key distribution, focusing on intercept–resend attacks against BB84 and Trojan‑horse attacks that exploit information leakage from quantum devices. The task involved measuring how attacks affect security, evaluating detection and defense strategies, and analyzing trade‑offs, with optional extensions covering additional device attacks and adversarial attacks on quantum machine learning models.
